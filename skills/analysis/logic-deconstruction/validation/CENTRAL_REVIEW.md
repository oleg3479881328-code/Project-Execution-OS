# Central Review

## Skill

`logic-deconstruction`

## Review Scope

Central review after migration into `Project-Execution-OS`.

## Strengths

- Clear task boundary: structured reasoning quality control, not generic brainstorming.
- Strong fit for design review, evidence separation, and anti-speculation governance.
- Workflow explicitly forces facts, assumptions, falsifiability, and competing hypotheses.
- Useful across software and non-software projects, which matches the universal OS goal.

## Risks

- The skill can be misused as rhetorical skepticism if operators do not anchor claims to evidence gaps.
- Overuse on simple factual tasks may slow execution without adding signal.
