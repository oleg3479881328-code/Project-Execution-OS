# Analytics and Experiments

## Purpose

Provide a reusable measurement loop for YouTube growth, format testing, and business review.

## Core Rule

Do not judge a channel by isolated views. Track patterns across formats, topics, hooks, languages, and business outcomes.

## Core Metrics

Track:

- views;
- impressions;
- click-through rate;
- average view duration;
- average percentage viewed;
- audience retention;
- watch time;
- subscribers gained;
- returning viewers;
- traffic source;
- comments;
- likes;
- shares;
- affiliate clicks;
- conversions;
- revenue;
- production cost.

## Shorts Review

Track:

- first-frame clarity;
- viewed vs swiped away where available;
- completion rate;
- loop behavior;
- subscribers gained;
- repeatable topic patterns;
- platform-to-product clicks.

## Long-Form Review

Track:

- title/thumbnail click-through rate;
- first 30-second retention;
- major retention drops;
- chapter performance;
- comments/questions;
- next-video flow;
- playlist contribution;
- conversions.

## Experiment Log

For every experiment record:

- hypothesis;
- channel;
- format;
- topic;
- hook type;
- duration;
- title;
- thumbnail pattern;
- CTA;
- publish date;
- result;
- conclusion;
- next action.

## Testing Rule

Change one major variable at a time when possible.

Examples:

- hook;
- length;
- topic;
- thumbnail;
- title structure;
- CTA;
- language;
- publishing cadence.

## Final Rule

Analytics exists to improve the content system. Record conclusions, not only numbers.
