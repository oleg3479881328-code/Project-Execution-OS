# YouTube Product Surfaces

## Purpose

Map recurring YouTube use cases so agents choose the right strategy before choosing production tools.

## Surface 1 — Shorts-First Channel

Use when rapid format testing, reach, and frequent publishing matter.

Typical parts:

- repeatable short format;
- strong first-frame hook;
- vertical video;
- captions;
- channel niche;
- analytics loop;
- path to long-form, affiliate, or product conversion.

## Surface 2 — Long-Form Authority Channel

Use when trust, education, deeper explanation, or search discovery matter.

Typical parts:

- research;
- script;
- title/thumbnail package;
- chapter structure;
- retention review;
- playlist strategy;
- call to action.

## Surface 3 — Hybrid Shorts + Long-Form Channel

Use when Shorts attract discovery and long-form builds authority, monetization depth, or product trust.

Typical parts:

- short-form discovery layer;
- long-form depth layer;
- shared niche;
- content repurposing;
- playlist architecture;
- conversion path.

## Surface 4 — Affiliate Channel

Use when videos recommend products, tools, or services.

Typical parts:

- product research;
- original commentary;
- product footage or owned/allowed visuals;
- tracked links;
- disclosure;
- conversion tracking.

## Surface 5 — Educational / QuizLight Channel

Use when YouTube teaches language, memory, or study workflows.

Typical parts:

- lesson format;
- transcript;
- phrase extraction;
- examples;
- card creation CTA;
- QuizLight integration;
- playlists by topic or level.

## Surface 6 — Documentary / Facts Channel

Use when original scripts, verified facts, narration, and edited visuals create value.

Typical parts:

- source research;
- fact verification;
- script;
- voiceover;
- visuals;
- edit;
- citations/notes where appropriate;
- originality review.

## Surface 7 — SaaS Acquisition Channel

Use when YouTube attracts users into a product.

Typical parts:

- educational content;
- product demos;
- case studies;
- comparison videos;
- links;
- lead tracking;
- conversion analytics.

## Surface 8 — Multilingual Channel Network

Use when one content system is localized across languages.

Typical parts:

- master idea/script;
- translation;
- local-language QA;
- localized voice;
- localized captions;
- localized metadata;
- channel/account architecture;
- performance comparison by language.

## Surface 9 — Internal YouTube Research / Transcript Tool

Use when YouTube is a source for market research, language learning, or knowledge extraction.

Typical parts:

- video URL intake;
- transcript retrieval;
- timestamp mapping;
- clip/reference extraction;
- notes;
- downstream card or research artifact.

## Final Rule

Choose the YouTube surface first. The surface determines content format, channel structure, monetization path, production pipeline, and analytics model.
