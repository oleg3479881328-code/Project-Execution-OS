# YouTube Monetization

## Purpose

Provide reusable monetization guidance for YouTube channels and YouTube-driven businesses.

## Core Rule

Do not build only for ad revenue. Use YouTube as a channel with multiple business paths.

## Monetization Paths

### YouTube Partner Program

Use when the channel qualifies and follows monetization policies.

Potential earning surfaces include:

- Watch Page Ads;
- Shorts Feed Ads;
- Memberships;
- Supers;
- Shopping;
- additional YouTube monetization modules when available.

Keep eligibility thresholds and feature availability in dated research or official references because they may change.

### Affiliate Revenue

Use when videos recommend products or services.

Typical flow:

`video -> description/pinned comment -> tracked link -> merchant/network -> commission`

Good for:

- gadgets;
- software;
- tools;
- product comparisons;
- creator recommendations.

### SaaS Acquisition

Use when YouTube attracts users into a software product.

Good for:

- QuizLight;
- portfolio builder;
- browser extensions;
- video factory SaaS;
- AI tools.

### Digital Products

Use when viewers can buy:

- templates;
- courses;
- guides;
- prompt packs;
- research packs;
- language-learning products.

### Sponsorship

Use when niche audience quality is attractive to brands.

### Services / White Label

Use when content attracts clients for:

- video production;
- channel management;
- short-form repurposing;
- multilingual content factories;
- SaaS implementation.

## Revenue Tracking

Track:

- video id;
- channel;
- format;
- topic;
- language;
- views;
- watch time;
- subscribers gained;
- affiliate clicks;
- conversions;
- YPP revenue;
- product revenue;
- sponsorship revenue;
- production cost;
- net result.

## Final Rule

Choose the business model before scaling production. Views without a revenue path are a signal, not a business.
