# Bookkeeping Standard

## Purpose

Define a reusable minimum standard for transaction capture, reconciliation, records, and books quality.

## Core Rule

Good tax work starts with reliable books. Do not begin with deductions. Begin with complete transaction capture and reconciliation.

## Minimum Bookkeeping System

Maintain:

- a dedicated business bank account where appropriate;
- dedicated business credit cards where appropriate;
- a chart of accounts;
- transaction categorization;
- source-document retention;
- income-channel reconciliation;
- expense-channel reconciliation;
- owner contribution and distribution tracking;
- asset and loan tracking;
- mileage tracking when vehicle use matters;
- contractor and payroll records;
- sales-tax tracking when applicable;
- monthly reconciliation.

## Monthly Workflow

1. Import or collect all transactions.
2. Reconcile bank accounts.
3. Reconcile credit cards.
4. Reconcile payment processors and marketplaces.
5. Categorize uncategorized transactions.
6. Separate personal and business items.
7. Review duplicate or missing income.
8. Review major expenses and fixed assets.
9. Review owner draws, contributions, reimbursements, and loans.
10. Review contractor payments.
11. Review payroll postings.
12. Review sales tax collected and remitted.
13. Preserve supporting documents.
14. Lock or document the close date.

## Books Quality Levels

### Level 0 — Missing

No reliable transaction system exists.

### Level 1 — Captured

Most transactions are present, but reconciliations or documents are incomplete.

### Level 2 — Reconciled

Core accounts and payment channels reconcile for the period.

### Level 3 — Reviewed

Books are reconciled, unusual items are resolved, and supporting records are organized.

### Level 4 — Handoff Ready

Books are reviewed, open questions are documented, and the professional package is complete.

## Recordkeeping Rule

Preserve enough documentation to support income, expenses, assets, payroll, contractor payments, and sales-tax positions. Confirm current retention requirements through official sources and professional review when needed.

## Personal and Business Separation

Do not hide mixed-use transactions. Record them accurately, document the business portion, and preserve the allocation method.

## Vehicle Use

For vehicle-related workflows, preserve contemporaneous mileage or expense records, business purpose, dates, and supporting evidence. Verify the current tax-year treatment before recommending a method.

## Output

Produce:

- books-quality score;
- missing-data list;
- reconciliation status;
- unresolved transactions;
- recordkeeping gaps;
- next close date;
- handoff-readiness status.
