# Ready Solutions

## Purpose

Provide simple starting patterns for common workflows.

## Gig Economy Side Income

Start with:

- income channels;
- platform payout records;
- expense capture;
- mileage log;
- vehicle-use records;
- estimated-tax review;
- state and local routing;
- year-end handoff.

## Solo SaaS Founder

Start with:

- entity and tax-classification review;
- business bank account;
- bookkeeping ledger;
- payment-processor reconciliation;
- contractor records;
- digital-product and sales-tax routing;
- monthly close;
- quarterly review;
- year-end handoff.

## Small Service Business With Employees

Start with:

- entity review;
- bookkeeping setup;
- payroll software or provider;
- federal, state, and local payroll registration;
- worker onboarding;
- monthly reconciliation;
- payroll calendar;
- contractor review;
- quarterly review;
- year-end handoff.

## Ecommerce or Digital Products

Start with:

- product and service classification;
- marketplace and direct-sales separation;
- state-by-state sales review;
- payment-processor reconciliation;
- inventory review where relevant;
- sales-tax review;
- monthly close;
- specialist escalation where exposure is unclear.

## Catch-Up Bookkeeping

Start with:

- list all accounts and channels;
- collect statements and exports;
- reconstruct transactions;
- reconcile month by month;
- separate personal and business transactions;
- identify missing records;
- classify unresolved items;
- prepare a professional handoff if prior filings may be affected.

## Notice Triage

Start with:

- identify the authority;
- preserve the complete notice in the secure project layer;
- record the response deadline;
- identify the tax year and period;
- compare the notice to available records;
- gather relevant records;
- route to the correct professional;
- track response status.

## Final Rule

These patterns are starting routes, not substitutes for current-source verification or professional review.
