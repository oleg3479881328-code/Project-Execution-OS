## State and Local Sources

For each state or locality, use:

- department of revenue or taxation;
- secretary of state;
- labor and unemployment agency;
- official sales-tax registration portal;
- official municipal or local tax authority;
- official business-license portal.

## Source Maintenance Rule

Verify every URL, page date, tax year, form version, and jurisdiction before relying on it. Add dated research snapshots when rules, thresholds, or portals change.
