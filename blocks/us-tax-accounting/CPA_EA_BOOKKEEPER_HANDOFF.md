# CPA, EA, Bookkeeper, Payroll Specialist, and Tax Attorney Handoff

## Purpose

Route work to the correct professional with an organized summary and document checklist.

## Role Separation

### Bookkeeper

Use for recurring transaction capture, categorization, reconciliation, and record organization.

### Accountant

Use for accounting treatment, reporting structure, close quality, and financial statements.

### Tax Preparer

Use for return preparation and filing when the facts are sufficiently organized.

### CPA

Use for complex accounting, tax planning, entity analysis, multi-state questions, and material reporting judgment.

### EA

Use for federal tax matters and IRS representation where an enrolled agent is appropriate.

### Payroll Specialist

Use for payroll setup, deposits, returns, corrections, and multi-state payroll operations.

### Tax Attorney

Use for legal interpretation, controversy, disputes, appeals, collection risk, audit defense, and material exposure.

## Handoff Summary

Prepare:

- taxpayer and entity summary;
- tax year or period;
- jurisdiction matrix;
- books-quality score;
- reconciliation status;
- income-channel list;
- expense summary;
- payroll status;
- contractor status;
- sales-tax status;
- asset and vehicle summary;
- deadline calendar;
- unresolved questions;
- risk list;
- source links;
- requested professional decision.

## Document Checklist

Keep the actual financial and identity records in the approved secure project layer. Reference them from the summary instead of copying them into the reusable block.

## Handoff Rule

Do not bury uncertainty. State what is known, what is missing, what is assumed, and what decision is requested.

## Output

Produce a concise handoff memo and a secure document checklist.
