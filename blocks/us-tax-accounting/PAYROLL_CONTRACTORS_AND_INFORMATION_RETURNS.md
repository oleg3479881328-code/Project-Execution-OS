# Payroll, Contractors, and Information Returns

## Purpose

Route worker classification, payroll, contractor records, and information-return workflows.

## Core Rule

Do not treat every worker as a contractor by default. Worker classification is fact-specific and may involve federal, state, and local rules.

## Worker Intake

For each worker, record:

- name or secure worker identifier;
- work state and city;
- role;
- start date;
- payment method;
- contract status;
- degree of control;
- schedule control;
- tools and equipment;
- expense reimbursement;
- exclusivity;
- manager or supervisor;
- payroll-provider status;
- W-9 or onboarding-document status;
- year-to-date payments.

## Payroll Workflow

Check as applicable:

- EIN;
- state withholding registration;
- unemployment registration;
- local withholding registration;
- payroll schedule;
- tax deposits;
- payroll returns;
- employee onboarding forms;
- W-2 and W-3 workflows;
- benefits and reimbursement policies;
- payroll reconciliation;
- year-end close.

## Contractor Workflow

Check as applicable:

- written agreement;
- W-9 collection;
- classification review;
- payment tracking;
- information-return threshold verification for the current year;
- filing method;
- recipient delivery;
- correction workflow;
- state reporting rules.

## Information Returns

Confirm current requirements for:

- W-2;
- W-3;
- 1099 series forms;
- 1096 where relevant;
- electronic filing requirements;
- recipient copies;
- corrections;
- state copies;
- deadlines.

## Escalation

Use a payroll specialist, CPA, EA, employment lawyer, or tax attorney when:

- classification is uncertain;
- payroll deposits may be late or missing;
- prior returns are missing;
- workers operate in multiple states;
- local withholding may apply;
- corrections span prior years;
- an agency notice exists;
- large payroll-tax exposure may exist.

## Output

Produce:

- worker roster;
- classification-risk list;
- registration checklist;
- payroll calendar;
- contractor-document checklist;
- information-return calendar;
- escalation note.
