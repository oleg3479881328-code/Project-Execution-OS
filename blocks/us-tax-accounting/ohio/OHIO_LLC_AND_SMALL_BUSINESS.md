# Ohio LLC and Small Business

## Purpose

Provide an Ohio-specific checklist for sole proprietors, LLC owners, and small businesses.

## Core Rule

Separate Ohio entity registration, federal tax classification, Ohio state tax accounts, municipal obligations, payroll, unemployment, and sales tax.

## Intake

Record:

- legal entity name;
- formation state;
- Ohio business location;
- owners;
- federal tax classification;
- EIN status;
- Ohio Secretary of State status;
- products and services;
- employees and contractors;
- sales channels;
- municipalities where business activity occurs;
- state tax accounts;
- municipal accounts;
- payroll provider;
- sales-tax permit status;
- notices;
- known deadlines.

## Workflow

1. Confirm legal entity and federal tax classification.
2. Confirm Ohio business-registration status.
3. Identify Ohio Department of Taxation accounts that may apply.
4. Identify residence, business, and work municipalities.
5. Route municipal net-profit and withholding questions.
6. Route payroll and unemployment questions.
7. Route sales-tax questions.
8. Build a source-verified deadline list.
9. Prepare a professional handoff where needed.

## Output

Produce:

- entity summary;
- Ohio registration checklist;
- state-account checklist;
- municipal matrix;
- payroll and unemployment route;
- sales-tax route;
- deadline checklist;
- unresolved questions.
