# Ohio Tax Source Map

## Purpose

Keep a compact map of official Ohio tax sources.

## State Tax

Use the Ohio Department of Taxation for state-level tax questions:

- https://tax.ohio.gov/

Confirm the exact tax type, year, form, and filing period.

## Municipal Tax

Use RITA when the municipality is served by the Regional Income Tax Agency:

- https://www.ritaohio.com/

Use CCA when the municipality is served by the Central Collection Agency:

- https://www.ccaohio.gov/

Some municipalities collect directly. Verify the exact city and collector before answering.

## Employer and Unemployment

Use current Ohio employer and unemployment guidance:

- https://unemployment.ohio.gov/
- https://jfs.ohio.gov/

## Business Registration

Use the Ohio Secretary of State for entity formation and registration questions:

- https://www.ohiosos.gov/businesses/

## Verification Rule

Record:

- authority;
- source URL;
- municipality;
- collector;
- tax year;
- filing period;
- retrieval date;
- unresolved questions.
