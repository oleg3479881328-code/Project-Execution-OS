# Ohio Gig Economy Workflow

## Purpose

Provide an Ohio route for DoorDash, Uber Eats, Instacart, rideshare, delivery, and similar platform income.

## Intake

Record:

- platforms used;
- tax year;
- Ohio residence city;
- cities where work was performed;
- payout records;
- mileage log status;
- vehicle-use records;
- parking and tolls;
- phone and supply expenses;
- other income sources;
- estimated payments already made;
- municipal payments already made;
- notices received.

## Workflow

1. Reconcile platform payout records to deposits.
2. Review vehicle and mileage records.
3. Review business expenses and mixed-use allocations.
4. Determine the federal self-employment route through the parent block.
5. Determine the Ohio state-tax route.
6. Determine residence and work municipalities.
7. Verify whether RITA, CCA, or direct municipal filing applies.
8. Review estimated-payment needs.
9. Prepare a year-end handoff.

## Ohio Municipal Rule

Gig work can create municipal questions because income may be connected to residence, work location, or business activity. Do not assume the state return resolves city-level obligations.

## Output

Produce:

- platform income summary;
- expense and mileage checklist;
- Ohio state-tax route;
- municipal-tax matrix;
- estimated-payment review;
- missing-record list;
- year-end handoff checklist.
