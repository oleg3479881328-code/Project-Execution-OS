# Ohio State Tax Routing

## Purpose

Route Ohio state-level tax questions before municipal or federal analysis is treated as complete.

## Core Rule

Ohio state tax is one layer only. Always check whether municipal, payroll, unemployment, sales-tax, or business-registration obligations also apply.

## Intake

Record:

- taxpayer type;
- tax year or filing period;
- Ohio residence status;
- Ohio-source income;
- entity type;
- tax classification;
- business activities;
- employees;
- sales channels;
- prior Ohio accounts;
- notices;
- known deadlines.

## State Review Categories

Check as applicable:

- individual income tax;
- business income treatment;
- employer withholding;
- commercial activity tax or other business taxes;
- sales and use tax;
- entity registration;
- credits and deductions;
- estimated payments;
- notices and balances;
- filing portals;
- forms and instructions.

## Current-Source Rule

Verify the current Ohio Department of Taxation page for the exact tax type, tax year, filing period, form, and due date.

## Output

Produce:

- state-tax category list;
- applicable account list;
- source links;
- filing and payment deadlines;
- unresolved questions;
- next municipal or specialist route.
