# Ohio Deadlines and Portals

## Purpose

Track Ohio state, municipal, payroll, unemployment, and sales-tax deadlines without hard-coding stale dates.

## Core Rule

Verify every current deadline from the official authority for the exact tax year, filing period, municipality, and account type.

## Track These Categories

- Ohio state income-tax filings;
- Ohio estimated payments;
- municipal individual filings;
- municipal net-profit filings;
- municipal estimated payments;
- municipal withholding;
- Ohio employer withholding;
- Ohio unemployment filings;
- sales-tax filings;
- entity-registration deadlines;
- notice-response deadlines.

## Portal Record

For each obligation, record:

- authority;
- portal;
- tax type;
- taxpayer or entity;
- municipality where relevant;
- filing period;
- due date;
- source URL;
- retrieval date;
- responsible person;
- status;
- evidence reference.

## Collector Rule

For municipal tax, identify whether the correct route is:

- RITA;
- CCA;
- direct municipal collection;
- another official local collector.

## Output

Produce:

- Ohio deadline calendar;
- portal list;
- municipal collector list;
- overdue-item list;
- unresolved questions.
