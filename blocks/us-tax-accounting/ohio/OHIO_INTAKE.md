# Ohio Tax and Accounting Intake

## Purpose

Collect the Ohio-specific facts needed before giving guidance.

## Intake

Record:

- tax year or filing period;
- Ohio residence city;
- Ohio work cities;
- business location;
- formation state;
- entity type;
- tax classification;
- gig-economy platforms;
- employee locations;
- contractor locations;
- sales channels;
- products and services;
- inventory locations;
- Ohio tax accounts already opened;
- municipal collector already used, if known;
- payroll provider;
- unemployment account status;
- sales-tax permit status;
- notices received;
- known deadlines;
- last reconciled month.

## Municipal Questions

Always ask:

- Which municipality do you live in?
- Which municipalities do you work in?
- Is the municipality served by RITA, CCA, or direct municipal collection?
- Has local withholding already occurred?
- Does the business earn net profit attributable to an Ohio municipality?

## Gig-Economy Questions

Ask:

- Which platforms pay you?
- Where do you drive or deliver?
- Do you keep a mileage log?
- Do you track tolls, parking, supplies, phone use, and other business expenses?
- Do you receive W-2 income in addition to platform income?
- Have you made estimated-tax payments?

## Stop Conditions

Escalate when:

- residence or work municipality is unclear;
- a notice deadline exists;
- multiple municipalities may apply;
- payroll may be incorrect;
- unemployment-tax registration is missing;
- sales-tax collection may be missing;
- prior returns may need correction;
- books are materially incomplete.
