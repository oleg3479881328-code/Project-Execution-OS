# Ohio Tax and Accounting Sub-Block

## Purpose

This sub-block gives `Project Execution OS` a narrow Ohio route for state and municipal tax workflows, small-business compliance, gig-economy income, payroll, unemployment-tax routing, sales tax, and professional handoff.

## Status

`candidate`

## When To Use

Use this sub-block when the task involves:

- an Ohio resident with side income;
- DoorDash, Uber Eats, Instacart, rideshare, or other gig-economy income in Ohio;
- an Ohio sole proprietorship or LLC;
- Ohio state income tax;
- Ohio municipal income tax;
- Ohio employer withholding;
- Ohio unemployment-tax registration or review;
- Ohio sales tax;
- Ohio business registration;
- Ohio tax notices;
- Ohio year-end close;
- Ohio-specific tax-accounting tool or portal selection.

## When Not To Use

Do not use this sub-block as a substitute for:

- current official-source verification;
- federal-tax analysis;
- professional tax advice;
- legal advice;
- storing confidential records inside the reusable block;
- assuming one municipal collector covers every Ohio city.

## Core Rule

Ohio tax work must be routed through:

`facts -> tax year -> residence -> work locations -> entity -> tax classification -> income streams -> workers -> sales channels -> Ohio Department of Taxation -> municipal collector or municipality -> unemployment-tax route -> sales-tax route -> deadline check -> action -> escalation`
