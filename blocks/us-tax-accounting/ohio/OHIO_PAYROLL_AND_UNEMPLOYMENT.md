# Ohio Payroll and Unemployment

## Purpose

Route Ohio employer withholding, local withholding, payroll, and unemployment-tax questions.

## Core Rule

Ohio payroll may involve federal, Ohio state, municipal, and unemployment-tax layers. Do not treat payroll as one filing.

## Intake

Record:

- employer entity;
- EIN status;
- Ohio business location;
- employee work locations;
- employee residence locations where relevant;
- payroll provider;
- pay frequency;
- Ohio withholding registration status;
- municipal withholding status;
- unemployment account status;
- prior payroll returns;
- notices;
- known deadlines.

## Workflow

1. Confirm employer identity and locations.
2. Confirm Ohio employer-withholding route.
3. Confirm municipal withholding routes for each applicable location.
4. Confirm unemployment-tax account and filing route.
5. Verify payroll deposit and return schedule.
6. Reconcile payroll postings to books.
7. Review year-end W-2 and W-3 workflow.
8. Escalate corrections or notices when needed.

## Output

Produce:

- employer registration checklist;
- municipal withholding matrix;
- unemployment route;
- payroll calendar;
- reconciliation checklist;
- year-end checklist;
- escalation note.
