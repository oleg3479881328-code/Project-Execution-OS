# Tax Calendar and Deadlines

## Purpose

Create a reusable deadline-check workflow without hard-coding stale annual dates.

## Core Rule

Never state a current filing or payment deadline from memory alone. Confirm the tax year, filing period, taxpayer type, jurisdiction, weekend or holiday adjustments, extensions, and any disaster or special relief.

## Calendar Categories

Track as applicable:

- individual income-tax returns;
- business income-tax returns;
- estimated-tax payments;
- payroll deposits;
- payroll returns;
- W-2 and W-3 workflows;
- 1099 and other information returns;
- sales-tax returns and remittances;
- state income-tax filings;
- municipal or local income-tax filings;
- franchise, gross-receipts, or annual-tax filings;
- unemployment filings;
- entity annual reports;
- beneficial-ownership or financial-account reporting where applicable;
- extension deadlines;
- notice response dates.

## Deadline Record

For each obligation, record:

- obligation name;
- authority;
- jurisdiction;
- taxpayer or entity;
- tax year or period;
- due date;
- source URL;
- retrieval date;
- filing portal;
- payment portal;
- extension availability;
- dependency;
- responsible person;
- status;
- evidence of filing or payment.

## Urgency Levels

### Red

Past due, notice deadline, levy or collection risk, payroll failure, or material penalty exposure.

### Orange

Due within 14 days or dependent on unresolved books.

### Yellow

Due within 45 days.

### Green

Tracked, source-verified, and not urgent.

## Extension Rule

An extension to file may not extend the time to pay. Verify the exact obligation and jurisdiction.

## Output

Produce:

- source-verified deadline calendar;
- urgency list;
- missing-registration list;
- extension note;
- responsible-person list;
- evidence checklist.
