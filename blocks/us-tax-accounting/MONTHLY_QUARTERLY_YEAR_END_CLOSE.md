# Monthly, Quarterly, and Year-End Close

## Purpose

Turn bookkeeping into a repeatable operating rhythm.

## Monthly Close

1. Import all transactions.
2. Reconcile bank accounts.
3. Reconcile credit cards.
4. Reconcile payment processors and marketplaces.
5. Resolve uncategorized items.
6. Review duplicate, missing, or reversed income.
7. Review owner contributions, draws, reimbursements, and loans.
8. Review contractor payments.
9. Review payroll postings.
10. Review sales tax collected and remitted.
11. Review large purchases and fixed assets.
12. Review mileage and mixed-use allocations.
13. Preserve receipts and supporting records.
14. Record unresolved questions.
15. Mark the month as open, conditionally closed, or closed.

## Quarterly Close

Add:

- estimated-tax review;
- payroll-deposit and payroll-return review;
- sales-tax filing review;
- state and local filing review;
- contractor-payment review;
- cash-flow review;
- accounts receivable and payable review where relevant;
- tax calendar update;
- professional escalation where needed.

## Year-End Close

Add:

- final bank and credit-card reconciliation;
- processor and marketplace reconciliation;
- contractor roster and information-return review;
- payroll year-end package;
- fixed-asset review;
- inventory review if applicable;
- loan-balance confirmation;
- owner-equity review;
- state and local obligation review;
- missing-document list;
- open-question memo;
- tax-preparer handoff package.

## Close Status

Use one status:

- `open`
- `conditionally_closed`
- `closed`
- `professional_review_required`

## Output

Produce:

- close checklist;
- reconciliation report;
- open-question list;
- missing-document list;
- deadline update;
- handoff-readiness score.
