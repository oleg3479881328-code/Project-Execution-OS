# Sales Tax and Indirect Tax

## Purpose

Route sales-tax, use-tax, marketplace, and other indirect-tax questions without oversimplifying nexus or registration duties.

## Core Rule

Sales tax is jurisdiction-specific, activity-specific, product-specific, channel-specific, and date-sensitive. Do not answer from a generic state list or memory.

## Intake

Record:

- products and services sold;
- digital, physical, subscription, SaaS, marketplace, and service categories;
- buyer locations;
- seller locations;
- inventory locations;
- employee and contractor locations;
- sales channels;
- marketplace-facilitator channels;
- direct-sales channels;
- prior registrations;
- collection settings;
- exemption certificates;
- prior filings;
- notices;
- sales volume by state and period.

## Review Categories

Check as applicable:

- physical nexus;
- economic nexus;
- marketplace-facilitator treatment;
- registration thresholds;
- taxable product or service classification;
- sourcing rules;
- local sales taxes;
- filing frequencies;
- zero returns;
- use tax;
- exemption certificates;
- resale certificates;
- tax collected but not remitted;
- retroactive exposure;
- voluntary disclosure or professional review.

## SaaS and Digital Products

Do not assume SaaS, software, subscriptions, downloads, or digital services are taxed uniformly. Verify the current official treatment for each relevant state and local jurisdiction.

## Marketplace Rule

Document separately:

- marketplace sales;
- direct sales;
- marketplace-collected tax;
- seller-collected tax;
- registration requirements;
- filing requirements;
- channels not covered by facilitator rules.

## Escalation

Use a sales-tax specialist, CPA, or tax attorney when:

- multiple states are involved;
- prior collection may be missing;
- tax was collected but not remitted;
- product taxability is unclear;
- retroactive exposure may be material;
- voluntary disclosure may be considered;
- audit or notice activity exists.

## Output

Produce:

- channel map;
- state-by-state review matrix;
- nexus trigger list;
- product-taxability questions;
- registration and filing checklist;
- open-risk list;
- escalation note.
