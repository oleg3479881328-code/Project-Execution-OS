# Music Rights Checklist

Status: candidate
Updated: 2026-06-06

## Purpose

Preserve evidence for every generated music asset and prevent unsupported usage claims.

## Before Use

Check:

- provider or local model;
- model version;
- generation date;
- subscription plan or contract;
- commercial-use scope;
- attribution requirements;
- redistribution and client-delivery limits;
- uploaded references, lyrics, MIDI, or stems;
- ownership or permission for uploaded inputs;
- platform-specific rules;
- similarity and quality review;
- unresolved risks.

## Preserve With Each Asset

Store:

- prompt and controls;
- input references;
- plan or contract evidence;
- output file;
- edit history;
- normalization and mastering notes;
- review decision.

## Boundaries

- Code license and model-weight license may differ.
- Marketing language is not a substitute for the active contract.
- Royalty-free does not automatically mean unrestricted redistribution.
- Commercial-use permission does not automatically answer every ownership question.
- Uploaded reference material must be cleared separately.

## Final Rule

Never promise legal safety from a model name alone. Review the exact plan and use case.
