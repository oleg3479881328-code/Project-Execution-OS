# Immigration Monitoring Backlog: PM-602-0199 And Marriage-Based I-485

Status: active candidate backlog
Updated: 2026-06-06

## Purpose

Track changes that may affect marriage-based Form I-485 cases after USCIS PM-602-0199.

## Official USCIS Monitoring

Check regularly:

- USCIS newsroom release;
- official PM-602-0199 PDF;
- USCIS Policy Manual updates;
- Form I-485 page and instructions;
- family-based adjustment guidance;
- field-office announcements;
- USCIS alerts;
- USCIS stakeholder materials;
- DHS clarifications.

## Litigation Monitoring

Search for:

- federal complaints challenging PM-602-0199;
- motions for preliminary injunction;
- temporary restraining orders;
- district-court decisions;
- appellate decisions;
- class-action complaints;
- AILA litigation announcements;
- CourtListener and PACER docket activity.

Important rule:

Do not confuse lawsuits about other USCIS policy changes with a PM-602-0199-specific challenge.

## Field-Practice Monitoring

Collect only de-identified operational signals:

- RFEs citing or reflecting discretionary scrutiny;
- interview questions about consular processing;
- held decisions;
- approvals;
- denials;
- field office;
- filing category;
- entry category;
- overstay or unauthorized-employment facts;
- representation status;
- date;
- source quality.

Do not treat forum reports as verified legal authority.

## Professional Commentary Monitoring

Track:

- AILA alerts and practice advisories;
- immigration-attorney articles;
- law-firm client alerts;
- webinars and conference notes;
- legal-aid advisories;
- bar-association materials.

For each item record:

- author;
- credentials;
- date;
- source link;
- factual claims;
- legal interpretation;
- recommendations;
- unresolved questions;
- conflicts with other sources.

## Priority Questions

Monitor whether USCIS clarifies:

- effective date;
- applicability to pending cases;
- standards for extraordinary circumstances;
- treatment of immediate relatives of U.S. citizens;
- treatment of K-1 entrants;
- treatment of ESTA or Visa Waiver Program entrants;
- treatment of DACA and advance-parole entries;
- treatment of overstays and unauthorized employment;
- RFE templates;
- interview standards;
- denial patterns;
- consular-processing expectations.

## Evidence Package

For every material update preserve:

- source URL;
- date published;
- date checked;
- source type;
- official or unofficial status;
- summary;
- direct effect on the current note;
- whether block files require update;
- whether attorney review is required.

## Final Rule

Treat this as a fast-changing area. Recheck before relying on any prior answer.
