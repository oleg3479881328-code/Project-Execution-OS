# US Law Block Validation Backlog

Status: candidate backlog
Updated: 2026-06-06

## Purpose

Separate promising research paths from validated workflows.

## Priority 1 — Federal Primary Sources

Validate repeatable research workflows for:

- United States Code section lookup;
- Statutes at Large lookup;
- Congress.gov bill and public-law tracking;
- GovInfo authenticated PDF preservation;
- eCFR current section lookup;
- eCFR point-in-time lookup;
- Federal Register document lookup;
- official Federal Register PDF verification;
- Regulations.gov docket lookup.

## Priority 2 — Courts

Validate:

- Supreme Court slip-opinion lookup;
- U.S. Reports lookup;
- federal rules lookup;
- local court-rule lookup;
- PACER case search;
- CourtListener opinion search;
- RECAP docket lookup;
- CourtListener citation-verification API;
- case alerts and docket monitoring.

## Priority 3 — State And Local Law

Create state-specific source maps as needed.

For each state validate:

- legislature and statutory code;
- administrative code and register;
- supreme court and appellate opinions;
- court rules;
- attorney general;
- agency directory;
- county and municipal code sources;
- legal-aid and bar-referral sources.

Start with states relevant to active work rather than attempting all states at once.

## Priority 4 — Practice-Area Modules

Create narrower skills or source maps only when repeated work justifies them.

Candidate modules:

- immigration;
- traffic and driver licensing;
- employment;
- housing;
- consumer finance;
- small business and contracts;
- tax;
- family law;
- criminal-procedure triage;
- intellectual property;
- AI, privacy, and platform compliance.

## Priority 5 — Professional Platforms

Evaluate when access is available:

- Westlaw Precision and KeyCite;
- Lexis+ AI and Shepard's;
- Bloomberg Law;
- vLex Vincent AI;
- Fastcase or vLex Fastcase;
- specialized practice-area databases.

Validate:

- coverage;
- citator quality;
- source linking;
- AI-answer verification;
- export options;
- monitoring;
- pricing;
- privacy and confidentiality boundaries.

## Priority 6 — Automation

Prototype only after manual workflows are validated:

- legislative alerts through Congress.gov API;
- regulatory alerts through Federal Register API;
- eCFR section-change tracking;
- GovInfo official PDF preservation;
- CourtListener citation validation;
- CourtListener case and docket alerts;
- legal research memo assembly;
- source freshness checks.

## Evidence Package

For every validation preserve:

- date;
- jurisdiction;
- source URLs;
- search query;
- result screenshots or saved PDFs where useful;
- official publication status;
- successful retrieval path;
- limitations;
- automation notes;
- result: promote, keep experimental, or reject.

## Final Rule

Validate narrowly on real legal questions before building broad automation.
