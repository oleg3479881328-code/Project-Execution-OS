# US Law Jurisdiction And Deadline Checklist

## Purpose

Run this checklist before substantive legal analysis.

## Jurisdiction

Identify:

- country and state or territory;
- county and city when local law may apply;
- federal versus state issue;
- court system and venue;
- agency or administrative body;
- contract choice-of-law clause;
- contract forum or arbitration clause;
- specialized jurisdiction such as immigration, tax, bankruptcy, intellectual property, military, tribal, or benefits law;
- whether more than one jurisdiction may apply.

## Dates

Capture:

- date of the event;
- date a document was signed;
- date a notice was received;
- date a notice was mailed or served;
- filing date;
- hearing date;
- response deadline;
- appeal deadline;
- cure period;
- payment deadline;
- renewal or termination date;
- status-expiration or travel date when relevant.

## Immediate Deadline Search

Check for:

- statutes of limitation;
- filing windows;
- appeal periods;
- administrative exhaustion deadlines;
- agency response periods;
- notice requirements;
- eviction, foreclosure, repossession, or utility-shutoff deadlines;
- criminal-court appearance dates;
- immigration filing, status, removal, detention, or travel consequences;
- tax assessment, audit, appeal, or payment deadlines;
- contract cure and termination periods.

## Documents

Request the full text of relevant documents, including attachments, envelopes, service proof, screenshots, and prior correspondence.

Do not rely only on paraphrases when the document language matters.

## Current-Law Check

Before using a source, confirm:

- jurisdiction;
- effective date;
- amendment history;
- point-in-time version when past events matter;
- court level;
- case treatment;
- local rules;
- agency instructions and policy updates;
- whether emergency orders or temporary rules apply.

## Output

Record:

- confirmed jurisdiction;
- possible additional jurisdictions;
- known deadlines;
- deadlines still requiring confirmation;
- urgent risks;
- missing documents;
- next safe action.

## Final Rule

Do not bury a deadline question beneath a long legal explanation.
