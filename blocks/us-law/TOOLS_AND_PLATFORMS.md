# US Law Tools And Platforms

## Purpose

Route legal research to the smallest useful source or platform.

## Primary Federal Sources

| Need | Default source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Codified federal statutes | https://uscode.house.gov/ | United States Code from the Office of the Law Revision Counsel. Use enacted law or Statutes at Large when exact enacted text matters. |
| Enacted federal laws and authenticated federal publications | https://www.govinfo.gov/ | Use for Statutes at Large, Federal Register, CFR, U.S. Reports, Congressional Record, and other official collections. |
| Bills, public laws, legislative history, status, nominations, treaties | https://www.congress.gov/ | Use Congress.gov and its API when legislative tracking matters. |
| Current federal regulations | https://www.ecfr.gov/ | Use eCFR for continuously updated editorial access and point-in-time research. Note that eCFR is not an official legal edition. |
| Published rules, proposed rules, notices, presidential documents | https://www.federalregister.gov/ | Use for discovery and research. Verify official PDF on GovInfo for controlling publication context. |
| Rulemaking dockets and public comments | https://www.regulations.gov/ | Use for agency docket materials and public-comment records. |
| Federal agency directory | https://www.usa.gov/agency-index | Use to locate official agency websites. |
| State and territory directory | https://www.usa.gov/state-governments | Use to reach official state and territory sources. |

## Federal Courts

| Need | Default source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Supreme Court opinions | https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/opinions.aspx | Use slip opinions and official U.S. Reports materials. |
| Federal court rules | https://www.uscourts.gov/rules-policies/current-rules-practice-procedure | Also check local rules, standing orders, and judge-specific procedures. |
| Federal dockets and filed documents | https://pacer.uscourts.gov/ | PACER is the official access system for federal court electronic records. |
| Searchable opinions, RECAP archive, alerts, citation checks, APIs | https://www.courtlistener.com/ | Useful search and automation layer from Free Law Project. Verify critical documents against official court sources or PACER. |

## Automation APIs

### Congress.gov API

Use:

https://api.congress.gov/

Potential uses:

- legislative monitoring;
- bill and public-law tracking;
- automated update checks;
- research dashboards.

### Federal Register API

Use:

https://www.federalregister.gov/developers/documentation/api/v1

Potential uses:

- monitoring new rules and notices;
- agency-specific alerts;
- topic tracking;
- regulatory change detection.

### eCFR Developer Resources

Use:

https://www.ecfr.gov/reader-aids/developer-resources/rest-api

Potential uses:

- regulation lookup;
- point-in-time checks;
- title and section retrieval;
- automated comparison workflows.

### GovInfo API

Use:

https://www.govinfo.gov/developers

Potential uses:

- authenticated document retrieval;
- bulk federal publication access;
- metadata workflows;
- official PDF preservation.

### CourtListener API

Use:

https://www.courtlistener.com/help/api/rest/

Potential uses:

- case-law search;
- PACER and RECAP data access;
- citation lookup and verification;
- citation graph analysis;
- alerts and webhooks;
- docket monitoring.

CourtListener is a useful automation layer, not a replacement for official verification.

## Paid Research Platforms

Use paid databases when the matter justifies faster research, citator review, broader coverage, or professional workflow support.

Candidate platforms:

- Westlaw Precision and KeyCite;
- Lexis+ AI and Shepard's;
- Bloomberg Law;
- vLex Vincent AI;
- Fastcase or vLex Fastcase where appropriate;
- specialized practice-area platforms.

Important rule:

- treat AI features as research accelerators;
- independently verify cited authorities;
- use citators for later treatment;
- preserve the source and date checked;
- do not treat generated text as authority.

## Legal Aid And Lawyer Referral

Use:

- Legal Services Corporation: https://www.lsc.gov/about-lsc/what-legal-aid/get-legal-help
- ABA Free Legal Answers and related ABA resources: https://www.americanbar.org/groups/legal_services/flh-home/flh-free-legal-help/
- state and local bar referral services;
- court self-help centers;
- public defenders where applicable;
- agency ombudsman or help resources when appropriate.

## Domain-Specific Official Sources

Use the relevant official agency source when the issue falls within a specialized area.

Examples:

- immigration: USCIS, EOIR, Department of State, CBP, ICE, federal statutes and regulations;
- tax: IRS, Treasury, Tax Court, statutes, regulations, revenue rulings, and official instructions;
- employment: Department of Labor, EEOC, state labor agencies, statutes and regulations;
- consumer finance: CFPB, FTC, state regulators, statutes and regulations;
- housing: HUD, state and local housing authorities, courts, statutes, and ordinances;
- business and securities: Secretary of State, IRS, SEC, state regulators, statutes, and regulations;
- intellectual property: USPTO, Copyright Office, federal statutes, regulations, and courts.

## Final Rule

Use official sources for the legal answer, research platforms for acceleration, and paid systems when the risk or complexity justifies them.
