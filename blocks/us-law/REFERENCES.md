# US Law References

## Purpose

Compact source map for United States legal research.

## Federal Statutes And Publications

- United States Code: https://uscode.house.gov/
- Congress.gov: https://www.congress.gov/
- Congress.gov API: https://api.congress.gov/
- GovInfo: https://www.govinfo.gov/
- GovInfo developers: https://www.govinfo.gov/developers
- Statutes at Large collection: https://www.govinfo.gov/app/collection/statute

## Regulations And Rulemaking

- eCFR: https://www.ecfr.gov/
- eCFR point-in-time guide: https://www.ecfr.gov/reader-aids/using-ecfr-point-in-time-system
- eCFR REST API: https://www.ecfr.gov/reader-aids/developer-resources/rest-api
- Federal Register: https://www.federalregister.gov/
- Federal Register API: https://www.federalregister.gov/developers/documentation/api/v1
- Regulations.gov: https://www.regulations.gov/

## Courts

- Supreme Court opinions: https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/opinions.aspx
- U.S. Courts rules: https://www.uscourts.gov/rules-policies/current-rules-practice-procedure
- PACER: https://pacer.uscourts.gov/
- CourtListener: https://www.courtlistener.com/
- CourtListener API: https://www.courtlistener.com/help/api/rest/

## State, Territory, And Agency Directories

- USAGov state governments: https://www.usa.gov/state-governments
- USAGov agency index: https://www.usa.gov/agency-index

## Help And Escalation

- Legal Services Corporation legal aid: https://www.lsc.gov/about-lsc/what-legal-aid/get-legal-help
- ABA free legal help: https://www.americanbar.org/groups/legal_services/flh-home/flh-free-legal-help/

## Paid Research Platforms

Evaluate when the matter justifies professional citators, broader coverage, or faster research:

- Westlaw Precision and KeyCite;
- Lexis+ AI and Shepard's;
- Bloomberg Law;
- vLex Vincent AI;
- Fastcase or vLex Fastcase;
- specialized practice-area systems.

## Domain-Specific Official Sources

Use the relevant official agency sources for the practice area.

Examples:

- USCIS, EOIR, Department of State, CBP, and ICE for immigration;
- IRS, Treasury, and U.S. Tax Court for tax;
- Department of Labor and EEOC for employment;
- CFPB and FTC for consumer finance and consumer protection;
- HUD and local housing authorities for housing;
- SEC and state regulators for securities;
- USPTO and Copyright Office for intellectual property.
