# Monetization

## Purpose

Provide reusable monetization guidance for video-production systems and content factories.

## Core Rule

Do not confuse views with revenue. Every content factory needs an explicit business model.

## Monetization Models

### Platform Revenue Share

Use when platform monetization programs are available and the channel qualifies.

Review:

- eligibility;
- originality rules;
- region/account rules;
- payout thresholds;
- content restrictions;
- platform dependency risk.

### Affiliate Revenue

Use when videos recommend products or services.

Typical flow:

`content -> CTA -> tracked link -> merchant/network -> commission`

Good for:

- gadget channels;
- tools;
- software;
- product comparison;
- creator recommendations.

### Digital Products

Use when videos lead to a paid product.

Examples:

- templates;
- courses;
- prompt packs;
- research packs;
- language-learning products;
- QuizLight subscriptions.

### SaaS Lead Generation

Use when content attracts users into a software product.

Examples:

- QuizLight;
- portfolio builder;
- video factory SaaS;
- AI tools;
- browser extensions.

### Sponsorship

Use when niche audience quality is valuable to brands.

### Client Services / White Label

Use when the factory is sold as a service.

Examples:

- multilingual reels factory;
- product video factory;
- short-form repurposing;
- white-label content automation.

## Revenue Tracking Fields

Track:

- video id;
- platform;
- account/page;
- niche;
- language;
- source;
- template;
- views;
- watch time;
- clicks;
- affiliate conversions;
- platform revenue;
- product revenue;
- sponsorship revenue;
- cost;
- net result.

## Cost Review

Include:

- editing time;
- TTS cost;
- AI generation cost;
- stock footage cost;
- render cost;
- publishing labor;
- review labor;
- automation maintenance.

## Final Rule

Build the revenue model into the pipeline from the beginning. A factory that creates videos without tracking business results is only a content generator.
