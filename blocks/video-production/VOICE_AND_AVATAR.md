# Voice and Avatar

## Purpose

Provide reusable guidance for AI voiceover, human voice, avatars, and anonymous-channel presentation.

## Voice Options

Use:

- human voice when authenticity matters;
- premium TTS for polished anonymous content;
- lower-cost or local TTS for testing;
- per-language voices for localization.

## Voice Review

Check:

- pronunciation;
- pacing;
- emphasis;
- emotional fit;
- language quality;
- background music balance;
- whether disclosure is required.

## Avatar Options

Use avatars when:

- presenter presence improves trust;
- multilingual presenter output matters;
- product demos need a host;
- the brand supports synthetic presentation.

Avoid avatars when:

- the result feels uncanny;
- a real person could be misrepresented;
- the avatar distracts from the content;
- simple voiceover is better.

## Anonymous Channel Pattern

Typical stack:

- topic research;
- verified script;
- TTS;
- stock/original visuals;
- captions;
- consistent brand template;
- no personal face or voice.

## Final Rule

Use the simplest presentation layer that supports trust. Do not add avatars just because the technology exists.
