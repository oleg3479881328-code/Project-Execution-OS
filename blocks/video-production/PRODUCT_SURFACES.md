# Video Production Product Surfaces

## Purpose

Map recurring video-production product types so agents choose the right pipeline before choosing tools.

## Surface 1 — Long Video To Shorts Factory

Use when one long-form source becomes many short clips.

Typical parts:

- source import or download;
- transcript;
- highlight detection;
- clip extraction;
- captions;
- vertical reframing;
- branding;
- publish package.

Good for:

- podcasts;
- interviews;
- lectures;
- product reviews;
- YouTube videos.

## Surface 2 — Gadget / Affiliate Reels Factory

Use when product clips are turned into monetizable short videos.

Typical parts:

- product footage;
- hook;
- benefits;
- captions;
- voiceover;
- affiliate CTA;
- link database;
- multilingual versions.

## Surface 3 — Facts / Cinema / Documentary Factory

Use when short videos are generated from fact-based scripts and media assets.

Typical parts:

- topic research;
- fact verification;
- script;
- voiceover;
- visual sourcing;
- edit;
- captions;
- publish package.

## Surface 4 — Multilingual Content Factory

Use when one content item is adapted into several languages.

Typical parts:

- master script;
- translation;
- localized voice;
- localized captions;
- localized title/description;
- per-language export;
- per-language platform publishing.

## Surface 5 — QuizLight Video Learning Factory

Use when source video becomes language-learning material.

Typical parts:

- video player;
- transcript;
- timestamp selection;
- phrase extraction;
- translation;
- surrounding context;
- card generation;
- clip start/end playback.

## Surface 6 — AI Generated Video Factory

Use when scripts, images, motion, voice, or avatars are created with AI tools.

Typical parts:

- script generation;
- visual generation;
- voice generation;
- optional avatar;
- assembly;
- review;
- platform adaptation.

## Surface 7 — Creator Assistant / Internal Editing Tool

Use when the owner needs an internal helper rather than a public SaaS.

Typical parts:

- desktop or web UI;
- source queue;
- clip queue;
- presets;
- ffmpeg execution;
- export folder;
- metadata package.

## Surface 8 — Video SaaS Product

Use when the production pipeline becomes a reusable paid tool for customers.

Typical parts:

- upload/import;
- user account;
- job queue;
- processing backend;
- AI services;
- billing;
- export;
- usage limits.

## Final Rule

Choose the surface first. The surface determines source handling, rights review, automation depth, tool stack, monetization, and validation path.
