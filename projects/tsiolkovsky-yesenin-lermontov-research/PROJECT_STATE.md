# PROJECT STATE

Status: active

Confidence: research core high for Tsiolkovsky identification and Yesenin; medium for Lermontov identification.

## Confirmed

- The strongest identification of the Gorky-targeted autobiographical text is the second redaction of K. E. Tsiolkovsky's `Моя жизнь`, created in 1932–1933 and expanded beyond 50 typewritten pages.
- A surviving typewritten copy bears Tsiolkovsky's own note that he had offered it to Gorky for publication and had not yet received a reply.
- The exact present location of the physical copy sent/offered to Gorky is not established by the open catalog evidence reviewed so far.
- N. N. Bobrov is directly associated in the source trail with use of the 50-page autobiography; identification of him as the exact 'second recipient' remains a hypothesis.
- The strongest match for Yesenin is `Собрание стихов и поэм. Том 1`, Grzhebin, 1922.
- Lermontov remains a candidate problem. Strong candidates: `Избранные сочинения в одном томе` (Grzhebin, 1921), `Лирика` (Mysl, 1921), and `Демон` (Neva, 1921).

## Explicitly not confirmed

- That the Gorky copy is already physically located in IMLI and merely uncatalogued as an attachment.
- S. A. Vlasov as the confirmed second recipient.
- A. R. Belyaev or N. A. Zabolotsky as confirmed recipients of this exact manuscript.
- The Lermontov title without an additional identifying feature.
