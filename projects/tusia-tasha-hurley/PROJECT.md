## Key Decisions And Constraints

- Existing Solution First: reuse existing project/database/site mechanisms before creating anything new.
- One central structured source of truth: the canonical Google Sheet above.
- Notion/Docs/research reports are supporting memory/evidence, not competing operational databases.
- One real entity = one canonical identity; preserve aliases and source IDs through crosswalks.
- Shared wedding participation does not automatically prove a direct repeat business relationship.
- Unknown date/venue/vendor fields remain unknown until evidence resolves them.
- No invented facts.
- Media is first-class; production readiness requires usable, provenance-aware media.
- Existing public URLs are assets; keep/redirect/deprecate decisions belong in Redirects URLs.
- Preview/staging is not production approval.
- Dry-run research findings must be reviewed/promoted before they become canonical facts.
- Preserve Tasha's real site navigation/experience; venue pages contain weddings and should not become a disconnected replacement site.
