# 08_KNOWLEDGE_EXTRACT — Green Apple Initial Definition

## Project-Local Knowledge

- Landing page projects must clarify audience before copywriting or design.
- Agribusiness landing pages can easily become too broad if B2B, B2C, wholesale, and investment audiences are mixed.
- Product line must be confirmed before page structure is finalized.
- Processing and sales should be treated as business positioning, not invented as operational facts.

## Reusable Knowledge Candidates

Candidate for central library later:

- Landing page workflow should clarify audience, conversion action, product line, proof, and CTA before design.

## Patterns

- One landing page should usually have one primary audience and one main conversion action.
- Agribusiness storytelling can follow: growing → harvest → processing → products → sales offer → trust proof → CTA.

## Anti-Patterns

- Building page design before audience and offer are clear.
- Inventing farm claims or product facts.
- Creating specialized agents before the project direction is confirmed.
