# 01_CLARIFICATION — Green Apple Initial Definition

## Ambiguities

The project is clear enough to start, but the landing page still needs definition.

Open ambiguities:

- audience;
- geography;
- real business vs concept/demo;
- product line;
- main conversion action;
- desired visual style;
- language of the landing page;
- whether the page should sell fresh apples, processed products, wholesale supply, or investment/farm story.

## Required Clarifications

Minimum clarification needed:

1. Who is the main audience?
2. What should the visitor do after reading the landing page?
3. What products should be promoted?

## Scope

In scope:

- landing page concept;
- message structure;
- product positioning;
- audience definition;
- conversion goal;
- landing page execution specification.

Out of scope for now:

- backend;
- payments;
- ecommerce catalog;
- real farming operations system;
- CRM;
- logistics system.

## Success Criteria

The first workflow run is successful if it produces:

- clear landing page direction;
- first target audience definition;
- proposed product line;
- first page structure;
- decision on whether specialized agents are needed;
- next concrete action.
