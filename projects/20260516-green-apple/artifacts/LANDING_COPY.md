# Section 2 — B2B Problem

## Section Headline

B2B Apple Buyers Need More Than Attractive Produce

## Intro Copy

For stores, restaurants, wholesalers, and food-service buyers, apple supply is not only about product appearance. It is about predictability, communication, quality expectations, and having the right product options for the right use case.

## Problem Points

### Inconsistent Sourcing

Buyers often need clearer supply planning before committing to future purchasing conversations.

### Quality Uncertainty

B2B buyers need product expectations that are easier to understand, compare, and discuss before ordering.

### Seasonal Planning Pressure

Fresh produce depends on timing. Buyers need early conversations around availability, product interest, and expected demand.

### Weak Supplier Communication

Wholesale relationships work better when buyers can clearly communicate product needs, volume ranges, timelines, and delivery expectations.

### Limited Local Value-Added Apple Options

Fresh apples are important, but many buyers also need value-added products such as juice and dried apples for retail shelves, cafes, restaurants, hospitality, and snack programs.
