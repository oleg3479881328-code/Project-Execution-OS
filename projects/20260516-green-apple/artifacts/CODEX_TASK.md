## Claim Safety Rules

Do not claim:

- existing farm operations;
- current production capacity;
- certifications;
- current partners;
- guaranteed delivery;
- existing processing facility;
- real harvest volumes.

Use concept-safe wording:

- building;
- preparing;
- planning;
- being developed;
- future supply;
- buyer interest.
