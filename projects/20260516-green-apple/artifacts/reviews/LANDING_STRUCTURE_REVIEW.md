## Summary

The landing structure is directionally strong and suitable for a B2B landing page concept.

It correctly avoids pretending that the business is already operating and keeps the page focused on Ohio B2B buyers.

However, the structure should be improved before full copywriting.

## Decision

`reviewed_with_required_improvements`

## Strengths

- Clear B2B audience: wholesalers, stores, and restaurants.
- Clear conversion goal: submit a wholesale supply request.
- Correct concept language: building, preparing, planning, developing.
- Strong section order: problem before solution, then products, processing, quality, local supply, partnership, and CTA.
- Separate Ohio supply section is useful for local trust.
- Separate processing section gives the project business logic beyond fresh apple sales.
- Good warning against fake claims about acreage, capacity, partners, certifications, or existing production.

## Weaknesses

### Weakness 1 — Hero needs sharper B2B positioning

Current hero is clear but could be more directly framed around B2B supply.

Required improvement:
Make the hero immediately say that Green Apple is preparing a local Ohio supply concept for fresh green apples, apple juice, and dried apples for wholesale buyers.

### Weakness 2 — Problem section is too generic

The problem section names supply uncertainty, but it should be more concrete for B2B buyers.

Required improvement:
Frame the problem around inconsistent sourcing, unclear quality, seasonal uncertainty, weak supplier communication, and limited local value-added apple options.

### Weakness 3 — Product section needs buyer-use framing

Products are listed, but not tied strongly enough to buyer use cases.

Required improvement:
Connect each product to use cases:

- fresh apples for stores, produce sections, restaurants, and food service;
- apple juice for retail shelves, cafes, restaurants, and hospitality;
- dried apples for snack shelves, cafes, catering, and ingredient use.

### Weakness 4 — Quality section must avoid unsupported claims

Quality is necessary, but the concept must not claim certifications, volume, production standards, or testing unless confirmed later.

Required improvement:
Use future-oriented quality language:

- designed around clear product standards;
- planned packaging clarity;
- buyer communication;
- freshness-focused supply model.

### Weakness 5 — CTA should collect B2B qualification data

The CTA form idea is good, but should be more explicitly B2B.

Required improvement:
Form fields should include:

- company name;
- buyer type;
- product interest;
- expected volume range;
- delivery location;
- timeline;
- contact details;
- message.

## Required Improvements

1. Sharpen Hero around B2B local Ohio supply.
2. Make Problem section more concrete for buyers.
3. Add use cases inside Products section.
4. Rewrite Quality language as planned standards, not existing proof.
5. Strengthen CTA fields for B2B qualification.
