Processing

From fresh apples to value-added products.

Green Apple is not only about selling fresh apples. The concept is built around using apple harvest potential to support value-added product opportunities.

Processing can support more product variety, shelf-stable options, retail and food-service flexibility, and stronger wholesale conversations.

Processing is presented as a planned value-chain concept, not as an existing verified facility or production capacity.
